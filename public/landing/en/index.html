FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is 5DO?
A resonance wellness platform that weaves together 150+ frequency & meditation tracks, biofield measurement, real-time cosmic resonance monitoring, and AI-assisted soul reading. Delivered as a PWA right in your browser.
Is the biofield measurement private?
Yes. Video and audio are processed entirely inside your browser and never sent to any server. When the session ends, all data is wiped from memory.
How much is free?
Four categories (Divine Tunes, Chakra, Crystal, White Noise), guided meditation, nature mixer, and the core frequency generator are free forever.
Does it work offline?
Free: The app shell and library metadata open offline once the PWA is installed. Track playback requires a network. The Frequency Generator and Guided Meditation modules work offline after the first load.

Pro: ⭐ Favorited tracks are automatically saved to the device so they play in airplane mode. Unfavoriting removes the cached copy to keep storage tidy.
What data does Soul Code AI use?
Your birth date, time, and place feed a Saju-elements + starseed + personal-frequency analysis, interpreted by Claude Opus 4. Personal info is used for the reading only and not stored.
What is QTX?
5DO's hardware line — Quantum Torus X — a full-band scalar-field speaker built with torus coils. In-app, the QTX A/B modal lets you hear the difference between regular 5 kHz-rolloff audio and QTX full-band playback.
