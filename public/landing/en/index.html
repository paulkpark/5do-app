FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use it without installing?

Yes. Just open 5do.app in your browser and start listening immediately. Installing as a PWA is optional — it gives you faster launch times and offline support.

Does it work with earphones and speakers?

Yes, all standard audio output devices are supported. For use with Quantum Torus X devices, the frequency output is specifically optimized for deeper therapeutic effect.

What audio format are the tracks in?

All tracks are mastered in FLAC (lossless) format, optimized for both earphone listening and PEMF coil output.

How do I use the Frequency Generator?

Tap the "Generator" tab at the top. Enter any Hz value or use the slider. Choose from Single, Binaural, or Dual-tone mode, and select your waveform type.

What is the Akashic AI feature?

Akashic AI uses artificial intelligence to analyze your energy profile and recommend personalized healing frequencies. It is available on the Premium plan (coming soon).
