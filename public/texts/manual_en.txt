1. Introduction

5DO (5th Density Oscillator) is a next-generation sound wellness platform that harmonizes your space through frequencies and resonance sound. It features core functions: Frequency Library, Frequency Generator, Soul Code (AI Soul Reading), and CE5 Protocol.

2. How to Access

Visit 5do.app in your web browser. No installation needed. Add to home screen for PWA app experience. Connecting via Bluetooth, AUX, or USB-DAC is recommended.

3. Frequency Library

Browse frequency & meditation sounds across multiple categories:

3-1. Track Playback
• Tap a folder thumbnail to enter a category.
• Select a track to open the player view.
• Control play/pause, stop, loop, and sleep timer.

3-2. Favorites
• Tap the ★ icon in track list or player to add favorites.
• Access favorites from the strip at the top of the library.
• Use the star filter (☆) button to show favorites only.

3-3. Search
• Search by folder name or track title using the search bar.
• Switch between folder and track search modes.

3-4. Playlists
• Create custom playlists from the player view.
• Add current tracks, import/export playlists.

⸻

4. Sound Mixer

Control sound layers with the 3-way toggle switch at the bottom of the player:

4-1. Nature Mix (Left Position)
• Select from rain, forest, ocean, brown/pink/white noise.
• Adjust the crossfade between track and nature sound.

4-2. OFF (Center Position)
• Play the frequency & meditation track only.

4-3. BGM Mix (Right Position)
• Select background music from Divine Tunes collection.
• Adjust the crossfade between track and BGM.
• Mixer is automatically hidden when playing Divine Tunes tracks.

5. Frequency Generator

A sub-tab strip at the top switches between two tools — Frequency Generator (make sound) / Frequency Meter (analyze sound).

5-1. Frequency Generator
Generate and play frequencies from 1Hz to 20kHz.
• Waveform: Sine, Square, Triangle, Sawtooth
• Binaural mode: Different frequencies per channel (headphones recommended)
• Dual-tone mixer: Play two frequencies simultaneously
• Harmonics: Add overtone structure for richer sound
• Presets: Save and load your favorite frequencies
• WAV export: Save generated frequencies as audio files

5-2. Frequency Meter (new in v3.9)
Live analysis of the fundamental frequency (F0) of ambient sound via built-in mic.
• Oscilloscope: waveform auto-scaled to ~3 cycles for a stable visual
• Pitch detector: autocorrelation algorithm covering 30Hz – 2kHz
• Tuner: nearest note (e.g., A4) + cents deviation (±50¢), green within ±10¢
• Log-scale spectrum: 72-bar view across 20Hz – 5kHz
• Use for: instrument tuning, verifying generator output, tuning-fork / singing-bowl measurement
* Audio is processed locally in your browser — never sent to any server.

12. Troubleshooting

• No sound? Tap the screen and check your output device.
• Playback blocked? Refresh the page.
• Weak output? Adjust volume or change output mode.
• Particle Life not working? Browsers without WebGPU (iOS Safari ≤17, some Android) auto-fall back to WebGL.
• Daily Resonance shows "no blueprint"? Run a Soul Code analysis first.
